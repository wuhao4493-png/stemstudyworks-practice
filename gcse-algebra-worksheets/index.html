    How to use these packs in a teaching sequence

    1. Identify the gap. Use a short starter, prior lesson evidence, or the free 10-question algebra check as an initial indicator. Two questions per topic cannot establish mastery.
    2. Choose the relevant topic. For a focused gap (such as reversing inequalities), a single topic pack may be sufficient. For three or more areas, the five-topic bundle is generally more economical at the listed prices.
    3. Assign a manageable section. These are progressive practice sheets; adapt pace and difficulty for your group instead of assuming every question fits every learner.
    4. Review worked answers. Use full workings to identify misconceptions and target the next session. These are original practice resources, not official GCSE papers.
